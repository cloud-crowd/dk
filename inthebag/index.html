Word set

Each set has 50 words, reused across all three rounds. Changes apply the next time you hit Start Game.

How it works

  1. Round 1 — Describe it: say anything except the word itself. No actions.
  2. Round 2 — One word only: a single word clue, nothing more.
  3. Round 3 — Act it out: no talking or sounds, gestures only.
  4. 30 seconds per turn. Pass the phone to the next player when it's your team's turn.
  5. One skip per team, per round.
  6. Tied after 3 rounds? Sudden death: one 30-second Act It Out turn each, no skips, most words guessed wins.
